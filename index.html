01 — The Man

Beyond the
Scoreboard

Mahendra Singh Dhoni is one of the most successful and celebrated captains in the history of Indian cricket. Rising from a small-town railway ticket collector to leading India to the pinnacle of world cricket, his journey embodies calm under pressure, fearless finishing, and quiet leadership. Known for lightning-fast stumpings and an ice-cool temperament in the final overs, Dhoni redefined what it means to be a wicketkeeper-batsman and captain in the modern game.

02 — Milestones

Career Highlights

International Career

Debuted in ODIs in Dec 2004 vs Bangladesh; played 90 Tests, 350 ODIs, 98 T20Is, scoring 17,266 international runs. Retired from Tests in 2014, from all international cricket on 15 Aug 2020.

Captaincy & ICC Titles

Only captain to win all three ICC white-ball trophies: T20 World Cup (2007), ODI World Cup (2011), Champions Trophy (2013). Captained India in a record 332 international matches (2007–2018).

Batting & Wicketkeeping Records

ODI average of 50.58 with 10,773 runs. Holds the record for most ODI stumpings (123) — the only keeper past 100 career stumpings.

IPL Career

Led Chennai Super Kings since the league's 2008 inception to 5 IPL titles, joint-most in IPL history. Stepped down as CSK captain ahead of 2024, handing over to Ruturaj Gaikwad.

2011 World Cup Six

His most iconic moment: the winning six in the 2011 ODI World Cup final, ending India's 28-year wait for the trophy.

ICC Hall of Fame

Inducted into the ICC Cricket Hall of Fame in 2025, capping a 14-year international career.
